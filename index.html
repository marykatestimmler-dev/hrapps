About

I’m Mary Kate Stimmler—a behavioral scientist who helps organizations make better decisions about their people. My work sits at the intersection of organizational behavior, I/O psychology, and evidence-based management.

I hold a PhD in Organizational Behavior from UC Berkeley (Haas School of Business) and bring 12+ years of experience leading People Analytics teams at Google. I’m the first practitioner fellow of Stanford University’s Center for Advanced Study in the Behavioral Sciences (CASBS), and I teach Data and AI Ethics at UC Berkeley.
